# P3: the comparison

P3 compares two runs of the same question (study QRO-0, sections 8 and 9.1). A comparison is a crate of its own: a
declaration, an optional copy of a pre-registration, and a record of its verdicts. `make` writes one; `python -m qro
compare` re-derives its record from the declaration and the runs' crates and holds the carried record to it, byte for
byte. The checker never passes what it has not checked: a comparison it cannot re-derive is refused, and what this
version does not build is refused `step-unbuilt`.

It is two builds. **P3.1** (this page) builds the design's items 1 to 5, 7 and 8 on IBM-shaped crates, four of its
controls and the plant of a fifth, and the stages `p3` and `p3-crates`. **P3.2**, after P5.1, builds units: crate
granularity, pooling across crates, the left-out rule, the readout bound rho and "isolated as quantum" with it, the four
controls planted in P5's unit twin, and M1's stage 4. The design is P3's (ledger, 2026-11-08T10:47:10Z, with its
corrections of 10:50:42Z and 10:51:27Z), approved by Logan on 2026-11-08 with the lead's recommendations.

**Every claim here is held by a test or says it is not.** The tests follow it in parentheses: a file of tests/p3 by its
name between `test_p3_` and `.py` (`declaration` is tests/p3/test_p3_declaration.py), a file of tests/p3crates by its
name between `test_` and `.py` (`p3crates_pairs` is tests/p3crates/test_p3crates_pairs.py), then its tests by name. The
names are held to the files (page: `test_every_test_the_page_names_is_a_test_of_its_file`).

`test_only_the_standard_library_and_qro`, `test_no_float_and_no_math`). Each function of P1's and P2's that P3's modules
call is pinned by its name and signature, and so is each method they call on an object of P1's and P2's they hold
(P1's Index and records, P2's law, contrasts and values), `manifest.circuits()` among them; the fields they read of
those objects are pinned by name; and no private name of theirs is used (independence:
`test_each_name_p3_calls_of_p1s_and_p2s_is_pinned`, `test_every_name_p3_calls_of_another_parcels_module_is_pinned_here`,
`test_every_method_p3_calls_on_p1s_and_p2s_objects_is_pinned_here`, `test_the_constants_and_fields_p3_reads_of_p2s_are_pinned`,
`test_no_forbidden_call_and_no_private_name_of_another_parcels`). The walks read names, not types: a call named `get`
is taken to be a dict's, as every one in P3's modules is; no test holds that.

## A comparison on disk

```
comparison/declaration.json        the declaration, qro-comparison/1
comparison/record.json             the record, qro-comparison-record/1
comparison/preregistration/<name>  the copy of the pre-registration the declaration names, any bytes; only then
ro-crate-metadata.json             generated from the three by qro-p3-comparison
```

**The declaration** is canonical JSON, written as the metadata is, and read strictly: exactly its keys, every value in
its spelling, each list sorted and each value once. Any other bytes are refused, and nothing escapes the reader
(declaration: `test_a_declaration_out_of_its_form_is_refused_and_nothing_escapes`, with its control
`test_each_hostile_declaration_differs_from_the_good_one_and_the_good_one_reads`;
`test_bytes_that_are_not_the_one_spelling_are_refused`). Its keys are `crates` (each run's pool of body digests),
`format`, `plan` and `preregistration`. The plan holds `alpha`, `family`, `granularity`, `inequality`, `kind`,
`measureSet`, `measures` (each a measure, its `distance` and its `epsilon`), `question` (`fixed` or `varied`), `runs`
(each a selector) and `sampling`. The plan's digest is the SHA-256 of its canonical compact JSON, and leaves the pool out,
since a crate exists only after its results (declaration:
`test_the_plan_digest_is_the_plans_canonical_compact_json_and_leaves_the_pool_out`).

**The generator** reads the comparison crate's own files and never the metadata file: the declaration, read strictly,
the copy named, and the record's digest and size, never its content (compare:
`test_the_generator_never_reads_the_records_content`). A file it cannot place, or a named copy it lacks, is its own
raise, a LayoutError, and a declaration out of form the declaration's DeclarationError; step 9 refuses each
`metadata-generated` at `./ qro:generator` (compare:
`test_the_comparison_crate_holds_exactly_the_files_its_declaration_places`,
`test_a_pre_registration_the_declaration_names_and_the_crate_lacks_is_the_generators_raise`,
`test_a_declaration_out_of_form_in_a_comparison_crate_is_its_generators_raise`). The metadata's `#comparison` is a
`CreateAction` and a `qro:Comparison` with `qro:comparisonKind`, and the root's description says whether the comparison is
declared real or a rehearsal, both body terms step 9 holds to the declaration (compare:
`test_the_root_says_whether_the_comparison_is_real_or_a_rehearsal`). No term of P3's is CHECKED, so the nine steps say
nothing of the record: `python -m qro check` on a comparison crate accepts a record no computation gave (compare:
`test_a_record_that_is_not_the_computed_one_is_refused_at_its_first_differing_line`, which checks it first).

**The record** is a pure function of the declaration's bytes and the runs' crates' bytes, in canonical JSON, every
rational in the profile's spelling and no JSON number but a whole count (record:
`test_the_record_is_the_same_bytes_computed_twice`, `test_the_record_is_in_its_form`,
`test_the_record_holds_no_host_path`). Its keys and their meanings are qro/p3_record.py's docstring.

## Runs and selectors

A run takes a pool of crates and one selector, fixed in the plan (the design's entry of 10:51:27Z). A selector names one
device, and may name bundles (manifest digests), jobs (job ids) and a window [from, until) of time. It selects each Job of
its crate that meets every key it gives (declaration: `test_a_selector_selects_by_every_key_it_gives`; runs:
`test_a_device_alone_selects_every_job_of_that_device`, `test_bundles_select_by_the_lines_bundle_digest`,
`test_jobs_select_by_job_id`, `test_a_window_selects_by_time_from_inclusive_until_exclusive`). A job's time is its
`startTime`, else its line's `submitted_at`; a job with neither is in no window (runs:
`test_a_jobs_time_is_its_start_else_its_lines_submitted_at`,
`test_a_job_with_no_time_is_in_no_window_and_selected_without_one`).

A member of a measure is a selected job holding a Pub the measure reads: role `law` for the law measures, and for `x0x1`
role `coherence` whose basis reads exactly qubits 0 and 1 out of Z, both in X (`y0y1` in Y). A control job is selected and
is no member (runs: `test_members_are_the_selected_jobs_holding_a_pub_the_measure_reads`,
`test_a_measure_reads_a_pub_by_its_role_and_basis`). Each Pub is mapped to its manifest circuit by its commitment (runs:
`test_each_pub_is_mapped_to_its_manifest_circuit_by_its_commitment`). A job's manifest is held where it is the manifest
of a Compile a Hold names, read by P2's own `qro.p2_measures.manifest_of`, the reading step 7 holds, imported and never
copied (runs: `test_a_held_compiles_manifest_is_read_by_p2s_own_manifest_of`,
`test_a_crate_holding_no_hold_has_no_held_manifest`). What the runs read is structure: no raw result is parsed (runs:
`test_reading_a_crate_reads_no_count`).

## What is not built

Refused `step-unbuilt` before any crate is read: a family rule, an inequality, a sampling model or a measure set but
`qro-family/1`, `qro-hoeffding-kl/1`, `independent-shots-and-jobs` and `qro-measures/1`; a distance but `abs-mean`; a
measure with no certified interval; granularity `crate`; and a run whose pool holds more than one crate (declaration:
`test_what_this_version_does_not_build_is_read_and_refused_step_unbuilt_by_name`,
`test_the_first_unbuilt_thing_in_the_plans_key_order_is_the_one_refused`). Refused `step-unbuilt` before the refusals: a
crate holding a `qro:Unit`, whose left-out rule is P3.2's (refusals:
`test_a_crate_holding_a_unit_is_refused_step_unbuilt_before_any_refusal`). And a coherence measure whose readout is
covered, below.

## The refusals before any value

In the study's order, each reading structure alone:
1. **`comparison-trivial`:** a member job of run a and one of run b share a job id, a PUB commitment other than `none`,
   or a raw result's digest (refusals: `test_trivial_by_each_identity_alone`, its three cases,
   `test_trivial_names_the_first_shared_pair_by_its_job_ids`, `test_a_run_against_itself_is_trivial`). `none` is never
   shared (refusals: `test_none_commitments_are_never_shared_and_nothing_else_shared_is_no_refusal`), and a shared control
   job is no refusal (refusals: `test_a_shared_control_job_is_no_refusal`; the control `shared-control`).
2. **`question-differs`:** a crate whose question does not carry the plan's measure set, whatever the plan's question
   says (refusals: `test_a_crate_whose_question_lacks_the_plans_measure_set_is_refused_whatever_the_question_says`); and,
   unless the question is `varied`, two question digests, a digest covering the law, the law circuit, the measure set,
   the twins and the decode rules of the member Pubs (refusals:
   `test_the_runs_questions_differing_by_digest_is_refused`, its three cases), or a measure whose member Pubs ran two
   logical circuits (refusals: `test_a_measures_member_pubs_running_two_logical_circuits_is_refused`). A varied question
   goes on, its cause DIFFERS (refusals: `test_varied_goes_on_with_the_question_differs`). The decode rules are read over
   the measures both runs have, so a run lacking a role is refused `role-missing` for it.
3. **`role-missing`:** a measure a run has no member of is refused by name in the record, with the runs that lack it,
   and the comparison goes on; with none left, the whole comparison is refused (refusals:
   `test_a_measure_a_run_lacks_is_refused_by_name_and_the_comparison_goes_on`,
   `test_with_no_measure_left_the_whole_comparison_is_refused_role_missing`,
   `test_both_runs_lacking_a_role_are_named`).
4. **`kind-not-qpu`:** a comparison declared real, with a selected job on a device that is not a `qpu`, `simulator` or
   of no stated kind (refusals: `test_a_simulator_in_a_comparison_declared_real_is_refused`,
   `test_a_device_of_no_stated_kind_is_not_a_qpu`); declared a rehearsal, it is computed and says so (refusals:
   `test_the_same_declared_a_rehearsal_is_computed_and_says_so`).
5. **`selector-late`**, kept: the comparison is exploratory unless the declaration names a copy of a pre-registration
   whose bytes hold the plan's digest as a whole token (late: `test_late_is_none_only_where_the_digest_is_a_whole_token`,
   `test_another_plans_digest_in_the_pre_registration_is_late`; compare:
   `test_a_pre_registration_holding_the_plans_digest_is_said_pre_registered`).

Their order is held (refusals: `test_trivial_comes_before_kind`, `test_trivial_comes_before_question_differs`,
`test_question_differs_comes_before_role_missing_and_kind`, `test_role_missing_whole_comes_before_kind`).

## What it computes

**The family M**, `qro-family/1`: over the measures not refused and the two runs, 1 for the pooled interval and k for the
members where k >= 2 (family: `test_m_on_hand_worked_cases`, its five cases, v0's 70 among them;
`test_a_refused_measure_counts_nothing`). A member with no shot in scope keeps its place and has the vacuous interval
(family: `test_a_member_with_no_shot_in_scope_stays_counted_and_has_the_vacuous_interval`). M and the record's shape do
not move when every count does (family: `test_every_count_changed_leaves_the_records_shape_and_m_unchanged`), and M is
counted before any value is read (family: `test_m_is_counted_before_any_value_is_read`, with its control
`test_the_poison_is_able_to_fail`).

**The values** come from the raw bytes by P2's `pub_values`, and **the intervals** are P2's `qro-hoeffding-kl/1` at M and
the plan's alpha, mapped (verdicts: `test_every_interval_is_p2s_at_m_and_alpha_mapped_from_the_raw_bytes`, ten cases;
`test_a_smaller_alpha_widens_every_interval`). The difference, the drift bound (two members or more) and the strong form
are P2's, exact; with one member there is no drift and no strong form (verdicts:
`test_a_run_of_one_member_has_its_pooled_interval_and_no_drift`, `test_a_run_of_two_members_has_p2s_drift_bound`). Each
total is held to a whole number or a Fraction before it is summed, and two Pubs of one job on two maps are refused (verdicts:
`test_a_total_that_is_no_whole_number_or_fraction_is_refused_before_it_is_summed`,
`test_two_pubs_of_one_job_on_two_maps_are_refused`). The means: `differ` if L(|diff|) > epsilon, `agree` if
U(|diff|) <= epsilon, else `unresolved` (verdicts: `test_the_means_at_their_boundaries`, ten cases). The record never reads a
crate's Measurements, Contrasts, `qro:alpha` or `qro:family` (record:
`test_the_record_reads_none_of_p2s_entities_the_trap_of_declared_values`, with its control `test_the_trap_is_able_to_fail`;
causes: `test_the_causes_read_no_record_of_p2s`).

## The check and make

## The controls

controls/plants_p3.py plants each in P1's twin T, with P1's toolkit, and T2, a copy of T whose morning coherence job is
re-identified: a new job id, new salts and so new commitments, one shot moved, the read's records renamed, every digest
resealed and the metadata regenerated. T2 shares with T its known-answer jobs whole and no member, and is accepted by the
whole checker (controls: `test_t2_shares_its_known_answer_jobs_whole_with_the_twin_and_nothing_else`,
`test_t2_is_accepted_by_the_whole_checker_and_is_another_crate`).

| control | the plant | comes out |
|---|---|---|
| `self-compare` | T against T, the comparison written by hand | `comparison-trivial`: the same job id |
