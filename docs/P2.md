# P2: the golden measures

P2 measures what a crate's law and coherence PUBs did. It says of each measure whether it tells the law from its
classical twins (signal) or only shows the run is sound (integrity), and it holds a compile's gate lists to the logical
circuits they claim to be. It is two builds.
- **The core (P2.1):** steps 6 and 7, every module they use, the seam with P1's generator, and the tests. The tests run
  on P2's own synthetic crates, which tests/p2/p2_builder.py writes, and on kingston's morning bundle.
- **P2.2:** the two controls, the tests on P1's twin, and the `p2-crates` stage on v0's two crates.

This page is the core's. The design is P2's (ledger, 2026-10-16T00:19:38Z), with the lead's decisions on it, on P2.1's
escalation and on its first two verifiers' reports (2026-10-26).

**Every claim here is held by a test or says it is not.** The tests that hold a claim follow it in parentheses: a file
of tests/p2 by its name between `test_p2_` and `.py` (`steps` is tests/p2/test_p2_steps.py), then its tests by name, or
a step's plants by their keys in quotes, each a case of `test_each_refusal_of_step_6` or `test_each_refusal_of_step_7`.
A claim no test holds says so, and why.

document out of that form (law:
`test_a_law_file_out_of_its_form_is_refused_for_what_is_wrong_and_nothing_escapes`, with its control
`test_each_plant_is_one_fault_in_bytes_the_reader_reads_otherwise`). v0's law is 78,482 bytes with SHA-256
`a242c4ded06168a7ee38d0b2d31e1b574c78c1cdf2633a4b2d6cecb5124ba3cd`: 4,368 layouts of 5 crystals on the 4 x 4 tile, over
4,096, with 1,360 forbidden; X0X1 = Y0Y1 = 3/8 (law: `test_the_law_file_is_one_canonical_compact_line_and_its_digest_is_pinned`,
`test_the_table_from_the_kernel_is_1360_forbidden_of_4368_with_weights_0_1_4_9_over_4096`).

## Step 6, `measure-class`

It re-derives, in this order, and refuses at the first failure, naming the entity and the term (each plant below is
held to both):
1. exactly one `qro:Question`, with `qro:law`, `qro:lawCircuit`, `qro:twins` and `qro:measureSet` (steps: "two
   questions", "no twins", `test_a_crate_with_no_question_is_refused_at_step_level`);
2. the measure set is `qro-measures/1` (any other is `step-unbuilt`), and its twins are `dephased` and `uniform` (steps:
   "another measure set", "one twin");
3. the law: a File of the crate, read by its reader, and the measure set's three needs of it (steps: "the law a rule",
   "the law's bytes out of form", and the three needs' plants above);
4. the law against the question's own circuit. `qro:lawCircuit` is a File in the question's `object`. It is
   simulated, and must lay every layout's probability, the mass outside them and each observable within 1e-10 (steps:
   "the law circuit outside the question's object", "the law circuit a rule", "the law circuit of another width", "the
   law circuit bent", "the law circuit out of the subset", "a law with one weight moved", "a law with one coherence
   edited"; the mass outside, hold: `test_mass_outside_the_layouts_is_refused_even_where_no_layout_moves_past_the_tolerance`);
5. the Contrasts: exactly one for each (measure, twin), each class and value as computed (steps: "a class declared", "a
   law value edited", "a twin value edited", "a vector measure with values", "a contrast missing", "a second contrast",
   "a contrast without its class", `test_a_contrast_of_no_measure_of_the_set_is_refused_at_step_level`);
6. the Measurements: every Pub of role `law` or `coherence` is measured from its raw bytes (steps: "one shot moved in
   the raw bytes", "a law pub read in another basis", "an outcome off the tile"; an Atlas law Pub,
   `test_the_honest_crates_pass_steps_6_and_7` on `atlas`). A coherence Pub whose raw result Atlas's rule decodes is
   refused at the Pub: that rule gives every Pub of a result the all-Z setting's layouts, so it holds no outcome in the
   Pub's own basis (steps: `test_a_coherence_pub_on_an_atlas_raw_result_is_refused_by_step_6`). The crate's
   Measurements are exactly that set, each term as computed (steps: "a measurement missing", "a second measurement", "a
   measure its PUB has not", "a measurement on the known-answer pub", "a measurement of no pub", "a measurement without
   its value", "a value edited", "the shots in scope edited", "an interval's end edited", "an interval's upper end
   edited", "an interval on a statistic", "the y0y1 value edited", "the n-crystal-share's shots in scope edited", "the
   nn-pair-correlation value edited"). Each certified interval is at alpha 1/20 over the family of every certified
   measurement that holds a value (measures: `test_the_family_counts_the_certified_values_and_each_interval_is_mapped`;
   steps: "the family edited");
7. exactly one `qro:Measuring`, with `qro:alpha` 1/20 and `qro:family` that family (steps: "no measuring", "a second

## The certified intervals, `qro-hoeffding-kl/1`

Hoeffding's bound in its KL form. For the mean p = T / n of n variables in [0, 1], the set
{mu : n kl(p, mu) <= ln(2 M / alpha)} holds the target with probability at least 1 - alpha / M. Over a family of M
intervals, the union bound holds them all at 1 - alpha. That is the theorem the rule rests on, which no test holds: the
tests hold the arithmetic to it.
- ln is enclosed in fixed point at 2^-128, with 41 series terms and a bound on the rest. Every rounding is directed
  (interval: `test_each_rounded_quantity_of_the_series_is_on_its_side_of_its_exact_rational_term_by_term`,
  `test_the_enclosure_holds_ln_against_an_80_digit_decimal_oracle`,
  `test_the_default_enclosures_integers_are_the_same_on_every_host`).
- Each end is a point on the grid j / 2^48, found by bisection, that is proven outside the set in integers. So no
  rounding can narrow an interval, and every host writes the same endpoints (interval:
  `test_each_end_is_the_last_grid_point_proven_outside_the_next_one_inward_is_not`,
  `test_each_end_is_proven_outside_the_kl_set_and_the_grid_point_past_it_is_inside`,
  `test_any_enclosure_is_sound_a_coarse_one_costs_only_tightness`, `test_golden_dyadic_endpoints_are_the_same_on_every_host`;
  independence: `test_the_intervals_module_imports_no_math_names_no_logarithm_and_holds_no_float`). Another host's
  endpoints are held by the same golden test, run there.
- A measure's interval is its per-shot mean's, mapped by the measure's increasing affine map (interval:
  `test_the_affine_maps_are_exact_and_increasing`; measures:
  `test_the_family_counts_the_certified_values_and_each_interval_is_mapped`).
- Every difference, drift bound and strong form after that is exact (interval:
  `test_pooled_difference_drift_and_the_strong_form_are_exact_on_exact_endpoints`).

The tests hold each rounding to its exact rational, term by term, at 2^-12, 2^-64 and 2^-128. They hold the
enclosure to an 80-digit decimal oracle on about 2,000 rationals, and each end as proven outside and two grid points
inward as inside. The golden endpoints are pinned, among them the design's measured difference: kingston's pooled
five-crystal share less fez's at family 70 is [12330612205551 / 2^48, 16819644152847 / 2^48], the study's
[+0.0438, +0.0598] (interval: `test_the_designs_measured_certified_difference_kingston_less_fez_is_reproduced`).
qro/p2_interval.py imports no `math`, names no logarithm and holds no float (independence, as above).

## The exact hold, `qro-exact-hold/1`

For each carried gate list, two checks:
- **(a)** the gate list's distribution over its clbits against its logical circuit's, every outcome within 1e-10 (hold:
  `test_a_plant_that_moves_a_probability_is_refused_a`,
  `test_a_gate_list_one_angle_off_by_1e_8_is_refused_a_at_the_tolerance_not_looser`);
- **(b)** the logical circuit against its role. A `law` circuit lays every layout within 1e-10, and the mass outside
  them too (hold: `test_a_law_circuit_that_lays_another_table_is_refused_b`, `test_the_role_problems_at_the_tolerance`,
  `test_mass_outside_the_layouts_is_refused_even_where_no_layout_moves_past_the_tolerance`). A `coherence` circuit's
  parity on its two basis qubits is the law's observable within 1e-10, its sign as well as its size (hold:
  `test_a_coherence_circuit_against_another_coherence_is_refused_b`, `test_a_coherence_circuit_of_the_wrong_sign_is_refused_b`,
  `test_parity_reads_a_pair_that_does_not_start_at_qubit_0`). A `known-answer` circuit has one outcome at probability
  1 - 1e-10 or more (hold: `test_a_known_answer_circuit_with_no_known_answer_is_refused_b`,
  `test_the_role_problems_at_the_tolerance`). A role that cannot be judged is refused (hold:
  `test_a_role_that_cannot_be_judged_is_a_hold_error`).

The gate list is read in the closed form of isa-gates/v1 (sim: `test_a_hostile_gate_list_is_a_sim_error_never_a_crash`,
with its control `test_the_hostile_gate_lists_control_is_read`; `test_bytes_that_are_no_gate_list_object_are_a_sim_error`;
`test_the_gate_lists_caps`). That this is the form Quantum-Film's own isa.py reads is held by no test, since isa.py is
not in this tree: the first verifier measured the two readers on 6,000 corrupted gate lists, and they differed on 3, a
width past P1.1's integer bound, which isa.py reads and this reader refuses. The logical circuit is read in the QASM
subset of the lead's E2 (5) (sim: `test_a_hostile_logical_circuit_is_a_sim_error_never_a_crash`,
`test_the_logical_readers_control_and_cap`, `test_v0s_circuits_read_and_share_one_core`), at most 100,000 gates and
100,000 barrier lines, each counted apart: exactly 100,000 gates and then a barrier line read (sim:
`test_the_logical_readers_control_and_cap`, `test_the_logical_readers_cap_at_its_own_size`). The simulator is binary64 on
the standard library (sim: `test_the_gate_matrices_are_their_definitions`, `test_tiny_circuits_with_exact_answers`, and
random gate lists and logical circuits against a plain dense simulator,
`test_random_gate_lists_hold_against_the_plain_simulator` and
`test_random_logical_circuits_and_their_pair_expectations_hold_against_the_plain_simulator`):
- runs of gates on one pair of qubits are fused into one 4 x 4, and a pair started on other qubits leaves a pending
  pair open (sim: `test_fusion_merges_runs_and_keeps_pending_blocks_on_disjoint_qubits`,
  `test_fused_and_unfused_runs_agree`);
- a qubit stays a classical bit until a block on it is no permutation of the basis with phases (sim:
  `test_the_light_cone_holds_classical_bits_until_a_block_spreads_the_state`, `test_monomial_is_tested_exactly`).

Kingston's four gate lists hold (hold: `test_kingstons_morning_gate_lists_hold`), every outcome within 1e-14 of its
logical circuit's (hold: `test_the_real_holds_are_far_inside_the_tolerance`); the design measured the worst at about
5e-17, a figure no test holds. The plants are refused (a): gate 303's sx on 47 made an x, gate 317's rz turned by pi/2,
gate 309's cz dropped, the coherence lists' h losing an sx, and the known answer's first x dropped (hold:
`test_a_plant_that_moves_a_probability_is_refused_a`). The phase plant is accepted and pinned (hold:
`test_the_phase_plants_are_accepted_the_stated_limit`).

**The memo** is process state, an LRU of 64 entries for each kind, emptied by `p2_sim.clear()` (sim:
`test_a_memo_is_an_lru_of_its_size`, `test_the_memo_answers_by_the_bytes_and_clear_empties_all_six`). Each key holds
everything its answer depends on, and each part of a key is held by a crate or a circuit that a key without that part
would answer wrongly:
- a gate list, by the SHA-256 of its bytes (sim: `test_the_memo_answers_by_the_bytes_and_clear_empties_all_six`);
- a logical circuit, by its width (sim: `test_the_logical_memo_keys_on_the_width_too`; hold:
  `test_two_circuits_of_the_same_operations_are_each_held_at_their_own_width`) and its operations, and its core by the
  core's operations (sim: `test_random_logical_circuits_and_their_pair_expectations_hold_against_the_plain_simulator`,
  whose 150 circuits share one memo); the coherence circuits resume from the law circuit's core (sim:
  `test_the_coherence_circuits_resume_from_the_law_circuits_memoised_core`);
- a comparison of a gate list with a logical circuit, by the gate list's digest (steps:
  `test_the_steps_memos_never_change_a_verdict`) and the circuit's operations (steps:
  `test_one_gate_list_named_for_two_logical_circuits_of_one_width_is_held_to_each`, one gate list named for two
  circuits). Its key holds the circuit's width too, which no test can tell from its absence: the gate list fixes its
  width, which must equal the circuit's before the memo is asked;
- a judgement of a logical circuit in its role, by the circuit's width (steps:
  `test_the_law_circuits_operations_on_five_qubits_are_judged_at_that_width`) and operations (steps:
  `test_another_law_circuit_of_the_same_width_is_judged_by_its_own_operations`), the role (steps:
  `test_the_law_circuit_named_again_as_a_coherence_circuit_is_judged_in_that_role`), the basis (steps:
  `test_a_coherence_circuit_named_again_in_another_basis_is_judged_in_that_basis`) and the law's digest (hold:
  `test_a_coherence_circuit_of_the_wrong_sign_is_refused_b`, which judges one circuit against two laws);
- the law circuit's hold, by both digests (hold: `test_the_law_circuits_memo_is_keyed_by_both_digests_and_cleared`).

A logical circuit is always simulated in two parts: its core, every operation up to its last x, cx or ry, and then
its tail of h and sdg. Once the law circuit is simulated, each coherence circuit runs its tail alone, from the law
circuit's core State, which the memo holds; and a circuit asked for again runs nothing (sim:
`test_the_coherence_circuits_resume_from_the_law_circuits_memoised_core`). Since the split is the circuit's own, a
distribution is the same bytes whatever the memo held before (sim: `test_split_takes_the_trailing_h_and_sdg`,
`test_v0s_circuits_read_and_share_one_core`, `test_a_circuit_is_the_same_bytes_whether_or_not_its_core_was_memoised`).

## What it concedes

Each limit names the test that pins what it concedes, or says that none does.

1. **The exact hold cannot see a change that leaves every Z-basis probability alone:** an rz on a qubit still in a
   basis state, or after its last two-qubit gate before its measurement. The phase plant is accepted; only the gate
   list's digest, which the manifest names, tells it from the original (hold:
   `test_the_phase_plants_are_accepted_the_stated_limit`).
2. **It does not tie a gate list to the QPY that ran** (`qpy-only`, P1's limit). No test of P2's holds it: P1's control
   `qpy-only`, accepted, is P1's.
3. **It is binary64.** libm's cos and sin may differ in the last bits between hosts, so a gate list within about
   1e-15 of the 1e-10 tolerance could be judged differently on two. v0's sit near 5e-17, and the plants far above
   1e-10. No float is ever written to a crate (contract: `test_no_float_is_ever_written_to_a_crate`). That two hosts
   agree is held by no test: one host cannot show another's libm.
4. **The law table is held to the question's circuit only to 1e-10:** a table that close to the circuit's law passes
   (hold: `test_the_role_problems_at_the_tolerance`). One weight moved by 1/4096 (2.4e-4) is refused (hold:
   `test_a_law_with_one_weight_moved_is_not_what_the_circuit_lays`; steps: "a law with one weight moved").
5. **The checker never derives the law from a kernel** (study section 10). A crate whose circuit and law table were
   both replaced consistently is another question: accepted alone, and P3's to refuse against v0's. P2's small crate is
   such a question, its own 2 x 2 law laid by its own circuit, and steps 6 and 7 accept it (steps:
   `test_the_honest_crates_pass_steps_6_and_7` on `small`).
6. **The intervals assume independent shots within a job,** which nothing tests. The forbidden share's and linear
   XEB's hold conditionally on the number of N-crystal shots. These are the bound's assumptions, which no test can
   hold.
7. **A crate's intervals are its own,** at alpha 1/20 over its own family, never a comparison's. Their ends sit on a
   2^-48 grid, each outward by at most 2^-48 and the enclosure's slack (interval:
   `test_each_end_is_proven_outside_the_kl_set_and_the_grid_point_past_it_is_inside`, which holds each end on the grid
   and two grid points inward inside the set).
8. **The NN correlation and the sampled TVD carry no interval.** Crystals per shot and the occupations carry a class
   and no per-PUB value in v1 (steps: "an interval on a statistic", "a vector measure with values"; classes:
   `test_c_the_vector_measures_carry_a_class_and_no_value`). The report functions are never CHECKED (independence:
   `test_no_module_of_the_package_but_the_report_functions_own_names_them`).
9. **Coherence measures exist only for X0X1 and Y0Y1;** a coherence PUB of another basis is refused, not measured
   (measures: `test_each_role_is_read_in_its_basis`).
10. **P2 measures none of Atlas's coherences;** they are the engine's REPORTED floats (P1's). Atlas's rule gives every
    Pub of a raw result the all-Z setting's layouts, so a coherence Pub on an Atlas raw result holds no outcome in its
    own basis: `contribute` raises on one, and step 6 refuses one at the Pub (steps:
    `test_a_coherence_pub_on_an_atlas_raw_result_is_refused_by_step_6`, on an Atlas-shaped crate of P2's own). No v0
    crate has one (the second verifier, READ); P2.2's tests on v0's Atlas crate hold that crate.
11. **A compile whose gate lists are not all carried says `withheld`,** and step 7 holds only those carried (steps:
    `test_the_honest_crates_pass_steps_6_and_7` on `withheld` and `mixed`, and "a carried gate list broken under a
    withheld hold").
12. **The memo is process state,** keyed by everything each answer depends on, which step 7 read itself: so no crate
    can move another's verdict, nor one entry of a manifest another's. Each part of the hold's keys is held by a crate
    that a key short of that part would pass ("The memo" above; steps: `test_the_steps_memos_never_change_a_verdict`,
    `test_one_gate_list_named_for_two_logical_circuits_of_one_width_is_held_to_each`,
    `test_the_law_circuits_operations_on_five_qubits_are_judged_at_that_width`,
    `test_another_law_circuit_of_the_same_width_is_judged_by_its_own_operations`,
    `test_the_law_circuit_named_again_as_a_coherence_circuit_is_judged_in_that_role`,
    `test_a_coherence_circuit_named_again_in_another_basis_is_judged_in_that_basis`). Tests that change the simulator
    clear it (sim: `test_a_circuit_is_the_same_bytes_whether_or_not_its_core_was_memoised`).
13. **Cost grows with the references, not only with the distinct gate lists and their width, and the caps bound it
    only loosely.** A reference is a manifest entry, read in each Compile whose manifest holds it (MEASURED at P2.1's
    round-2 fixes, on the second verifier's recipes; its own figures on the round-1 code agree):
    - each entry parses its logical circuit again, memo or not: one that names a pair of 99,990 gates cost 0.19 s to
      0.20 s a reference with every memo warm, nearly all of it the reader;
    - the memo is an LRU of 64 entries, so past 64 distinct gate lists the Compiles' references thrash it: K Compiles
      sharing one manifest of N distinct 7-qubit gate lists ran the simulator 64 times at N = 64, K = 1 or 3, and at
      N = 65, 65 times at K = 1, 195 at K = 3 and 390 at K = 6, the logical circuits parsed N times K at each;
    - the set of the crate's carried digests is rebuilt over every File for every Compile: 1.7 to 3.3 microseconds per
      File per Compile, 0.70 s for 2,000 Files and 200 Compiles.

    Larger sizes are BELIEVED. v0's gate lists, on 16 active qubits, cost about a second each (the design's measure).
    On 20 active qubits, the cap, a brick-work gate list of 416 gates in 76 passes took 32 s, and one pass on the
    state's lowest qubits takes 1.3 s to 1.7 s (MEASURED at P2.1's round-1 fixes, beside other work, so noisy). A gate
    list within the caps can hold about 100,000 passes, so one hostile gate list could hold the checker for some 12 to
    47 hours at those rates (BELIEVED, extrapolated, never run). Past 20 active qubits a gate list is refused, not
    simulated, and so is a gate list of more than 100,000 gates, or a logical circuit of more than 100,000 gates or
    100,000 barrier lines (sim: `test_the_gate_lists_caps`,
    `test_the_logical_readers_control_and_cap`, `test_the_logical_readers_cap_at_its_own_size`). The memo is bounded
    by its count of entries, 64 of each kind, not by their bytes: one 20-qubit logical circuit's entries hold about
    57 MiB (MEASURED), so a process that checks many distinct 20-qubit circuits could hold several GiB (BELIEVED).
    Running out is a MemoryError, the checker failing, exit 3, never a verdict (steps:
    `test_the_hosts_failures_inside_p2s_steps_are_never_a_verdict`). The costs themselves are held by no test. So the
    budget on the exact hold's work that the lead leaves open for P2.2's design (2026-10-26T18:56:48Z) must count
    references, not distinct gate lists alone: for each manifest entry of each Compile, a parse of its logical circuit,
    a comparison and a judgement, and a simulation wherever the memo no longer holds its answer.
14. **The measure set and the law are v1's, for Quantum-Film's pauli-4x4 question.** `question_files` refuses any
    other source circuit (contract: `test_question_files_never_reads_the_metadata_file_and_refuses_another_question`).
    Another question needs a new measure set version and law, never an edit under these names.
15. **A crate that carries no term and no type of P2's passes steps 6 and 7, whatever law or coherence Pubs and
    Compiles it holds.** Step 9 alone refuses it, wherever its generator writes P2's entities, as P1's do whenever
    qro/p2_measures.py is present (the lead's decision on E1; register:
    `test_the_stated_limit_a_crate_stripped_of_p2s_terms_and_types_passes_steps_6_and_7`, with its control
    `test_the_real_steps_refuse_the_same_crate_with_its_p2_entities`).
16. **An orphan gate list is read by no step of P2's.** A File shaped like a gate list that no Compile lists in its
    `object` or `result` and no manifest names is no compile's gate list, so step 7 never reads it, and a broken one is
    accepted (steps: `test_the_stated_limit_an_orphan_gate_list_is_read_by_no_step_of_p2s`, with its control: listed in
    the compile's `result`, the same file is refused, and an unnamed gate list listed in its `object` is too, "a gate
    list in the compile's object its manifest does not name"). Nor is a gate list read that a Compile lists under a
    name not ending `.gates.json`: step 7 passes over it (steps:
    `test_files_in_a_compile_that_are_no_gate_list_are_passed_over`). Step 9 alone refuses a crate that carries either,
    where its generator refuses a file it cannot place, as P1.2's does with its guards, which P1's tests hold and P2's
