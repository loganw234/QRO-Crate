# P5.1: the contributor kit, its local route and its crate

P5 is the contributor kit (study QRO-0, section 9.1): the tool anyone uses to run one free-tier unit, 18 jobs on three
devices interleaved three times, 479,232 shots, and get a crate for it that the checker holds as it holds v0's. This page is
P5.1's build, the first of the design's two (the ledger's P5.md, 2026-11-08T10:27:30Z, approved on 2026-11-08): the local route
and the crate, M1's stage 3. It ends with a local unit, built on IBM's fake backends and accepted by the checker.

Each claim names the test that holds it, or says that none does. Tests are under tests/p5/ (stage `p5`, the checker's
interpreter: standard library and git), tests/p5kit/ (stage `p5-kit`, which starts the kit's own interpreter as a child, named
by `QRO_KIT_PYTHON`) and tests/p5crates/ (stage `p5-crates`, against moth-quantum at 3a73823, named by `QRO_MOTH_ROOT`). **What
P5.1 does not build,** and P5.2 does: the key and `account.connect`, the scrub and the output scan, the pre-flight and its
writer, the re-fetch, the dry-run probe, and chain pinning (`--chain-file`). In P5.1 the real route is **present and
refusing**: no code of the kit connects to IBM.

## The recipe

`qro.p5_unit.RECIPE`, named `qro-unit/1`; its digest is the SHA-256 of its canonical JSON (`recipe_sha256`).

| | |
|---|---|
| devices, in order | kingston, marrakesh, fez (`ibm_*`; locally `fake_kingston`, `fake_marrakesh`, `fake_fez`) |
| a pass, on a device | job `control-p`, then job `film-p`, each submitted only after the one before is final |
| the order | pass 1: K.control K.film M.control M.film F.control F.film, then pass 2, then pass 3: 18 jobs |
| `control-p` | PUB 0 `known-answer` (X on 0, 1, 3, 7, 12), PUB 1 `known-complement` (X on the other eleven), role `known-answer`, basis Z on all 16, 2,048 shots each |
| `film-p` | PUB 0 `law` (Z), PUB 1 `coherence-xx`, PUB 2 `coherence-yy`, 16,384 shots each |
| the gate | a film job is submitted only after its pass's control job holds on both PUBs |
| shots | 9 x (2 x 2,048 + 3 x 16,384) = 479,232 |
| options | dynamical decoupling off, both twirlings off, `execution.init_qubits` on, `execution.rep_delay` the device's own default (0.00025 s on the three fakes); `max_execution_time` 15 s a control job and 60 s a film job (Logan's table, 2026-11-08) |

Held by tests/p5/test_p5_recipe.py (the totals, the order, the PUBs, the options, the digest and its divisor).

**The shots divisor.** `freeze --shots-divisor N` (the local route only) divides both shot counts by N, which must divide
2,048. The recipe digest changes, and the unit records it as `shots_divisor`, so a scaled unit is visibly another recipe and
never a unit of the first. The generator refuses a divisor on a qpu unit (GU2: tests/p5/test_p5_generate.py,
`test_gu2_a_shots_divisor_belongs_to_the_local_route_only`). It exists because one whole unit at full shots is long here: one film job at full shots
took 488.9 s (MEASURED, below), and the whole unit about 4,870 s from that (BELIEVED, from two points); the one whole unit built at
full shots took 7,982.9 s beside other work (MEASURED, below), 1.6 times the belief.

**Cost and spend, in `fractions.Fraction`.** v0's 65 jobs were charged 353 s. Model (a) charges 9/24576 s a shot, (b) is the
least-squares line 1.6871 s + 0.29741 ms a shot, (c) is 2 s + 0.35 ms a shot; a job's estimate is the model's seconds for all
its shots, rounded up. The highest of the three is the job's estimate: control 4 s, film 20 s, a unit 216 s (180 under (a)
and (b)). The resource-unit rate is 0.015434 per second (5.448135 consumed over 353 s: the account's own figures of
2026-10-14). The ceiling is 270 s a unit and the caps 15 s and 60 s, so a unit's worst case is 270 - 20 + 60 = 310 s of the free
tier's 600. Held by tests/p5/test_p5_recipe.py (the arithmetic, and that the module uses no float) and, recomputed from v0's
65 jobs and the account's usage, by tests/p5crates/test_p5crates_cost_constants.py.

## The unit directory and the crate

`freeze` writes, inside the directory it is given, a throwaway bare origin and a work tree (`WORK/origin.git`,
`WORK/records`), and in the work tree the unit: `unit/UNIT.json` and, for each device, `bundle/` (22 files: the five circuits'
four files each, a README and `manifest.json`) and, once it runs, `run/` (a job's marker, line, raw result, metrics, one record
a PUB and, for a control job, a verdict; or a status file and no result for a job that did not complete). The unit and its
bundles are committed and pushed before the first job, so the runner's anchor is the real one: a job's line is committed and
pushed before any result is read. The crate claims no anchor (limit 9).

`UNIT.json`'s SHA-256 is the unit's identity, printed by `freeze` with its first 12 hex digits. The crate's layout is the
generator's (qro/p5_generate.py, `LAYOUTS`): `unit/` holds the unit directory's files, and `2026-09-25/p3/circuit-ed767c01bd4b851d.qasm`
(where P2 finds the law's circuit by digest) and `LICENSE-quantum-film` are the kit's shipped copies.

### The entities

P1's shapes, so P1's steps 4, 5 and 8 and P2's steps 6 and 7 run on a unit unchanged. No checker step and no CHECKED term is
new. The root names the generator `qro-p5-unit`, registered in qro/register_p5.py (`GENERATORS`, nothing else), so step 9
regenerates a unit's metadata from its files. The new type is `qro:Unit` (`#unit`, with `qro:recipe`, `qro:contributor`,
`qro:kitTag` and `qro:unitJobs`); each device is an IndividualProduct with its `qro:deviceKind` (`simulator` for the fakes); a
job is a `qro:Job` whose `qro:kitCommit` is the commit `UNIT.json` names; a job that made no result has no Pub and no Decode.
Held by tests/p5/test_p5_generate.py (`test_the_twin_has_the_unit_the_recipe_describes`, `test_a_failed_job_is_a_job_with_a_status_file_and_no_result`).

A local unit's jobs say `qro:calibrationLink` `unexposed`, `startTime` and `endTime` `unknown`, and `qro:clock` `local`: the
fakes' snapshots are dated 2026-04-15 (kingston) and 2025-02-26 (marrakesh, fez), and a local job has no execution span. A
local unit tests the pipeline, not the devices (limit 4).

### The generator's guards

`GuardError` names the guard; step 9 refuses a crate whose records raise one `metadata-generated`. P1's guards are **imported
from qro.p1_guards, never copied**, and a P1 guard's refusal carries P1's letter. Every guard raises through
`p1_guards.hold`, looked up when it is called, so one monkeypatch of it switches all of them off
(`test_one_monkeypatch_of_p1_guards_hold_switches_every_guard_off`). A planted fault, made by the forger (controls/plants_p5.py,
`Forge`, which recomputes every digest a record holds over what it changed), trips each guard and no other first.

| GU2 | `UNIT.json` names the recipe and its digest as the constant gives it, the devices in order, the bundles' digests, one kind and one route; a divisor only on the local route | `test_gu2_*` |

| GU4 | each commitment recomputes from the record's fourteen inputs (P1's G4) after P1's G3 (`hold_device_runs`) | `test_g3_*`, `test_g4_*` |
| GU5 | the raw result has a PUB for each commitment with the manifest's shots, and the decode's counts are the record's (P1's G5); a raw result that is no IBM result is a GU5 raise | `test_g5_*`, `test_gu5_*` |

| GU9 | the read's records, where present, are P1's G7 to G9 (`hold_refetch_job`, `hold_refetch_result`, `hold_read_manifest`), all or none for a device, and only for a qpu unit | `test_g7_*`, `test_g8_*`, `test_g9_*`, `test_gu9_*` |
| GU10 | every file classifies, and no @id is written twice | `test_gu10_*` |

**A finding about P1's G7, and the refusal that answers it** (the lead's ruling of 2026-11-08). `hold_refetch_job` holds the
record's one `role` to the *set* of roles of the job's PUBs, so it cannot hold a read record of a job whose PUBs carry more than
one role, a film job (law and coherence). Handing it only the PUBs of one role would pass the job's other PUBs, their counts
and commitments, unchecked against the read. So GU9 **refuses** such a record: `GuardError` GU9, "not held yet"
(`test_gu9_a_read_record_of_a_job_whose_pubs_carry_more_than_one_role_is_refused_not_held_yet`). A local unit carries no read
records, so none of P5.1's is refused by it; a qpu unit's read can be held for its control jobs only (the tests' qpu read twin
has no film jobs). How a mixed-role job's read record is held, a record of P1's form for each role or a new version of P1's
record, is P5.2's design question with P1's. Nothing of P1's changed. P1's note 1 (the export test holds four conditions
elsewhere) stands.

**The pins.** tests/p5/test_p5_pins.py pins each name and signature P5 imports of P1's guards and readers, both ways: every
P1 attribute P5's two modules use is in the table, and every row is used. It also holds that P5 defines no copy of a guard.

**The seam with P2.** Like P1's generator, the unit generator calls P2's `contribute` only for a crate that holds files under
`question/`, and raises a `seam` guard for a module that is present without its functions or that contributes what collides
(`test_the_seam_with_p2_refuses_a_module_that_is_present_without_its_functions`).

## `kit-dirty`

qro/p5_tree.py, `tree_state(root, code)`, called first of everything by `freeze`, `drive` and `crate` (and by the fence). Dirty is
decided from the bytes against HEAD's tree, never from `git status`: D1 every path of HEAD's tree is a mode 100644 or 100755
regular file on disk whose bytes hash as git's blob id; D2 every other entry below the root, a directory aside, is a path of HEAD's
tree, whatever an ignore rule, `.git/info/exclude`, `core.excludesFile` or `status.showUntrackedFiles` says (`__pycache__` and the
root's `.git` are not walked, and a directory link or junction is an entry); D3 `git ls-files -v` marks every file `H`; D4 every
git call carries `--no-optional-locks`, `--no-replace-objects` and `-c core.fsmonitor=false`, no `GIT_*` variable, a null stdin and no
window, and the root is the top of its work tree; D5 the code running lies under the root; D6 HEAD carries a tag matching
`^qro-kit-v[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+$` (the lead's pattern), the highest of which is recorded. Held by tests/p5/test_p5_tree.py,
each refusal with its accepted twin: a modified, deleted or same-size-replaced file; a link in HEAD's tree; a clean filter, an
exclude file, `.gitignore`, `core.excludesFile` and `status.showUntrackedFiles` that make `git status` clean over changed or
extra bytes; `git replace`; assume-unchanged, skip-worktree and a sparse checkout; a `core.fsmonitor` hook (which starts sh, so
the test runs where the runner runs it, sbx-mid.sh); a `GIT_DIR` in the environment; a subdirectory, a plain directory and an
unborn HEAD; the code outside the root; an untagged HEAD, a stale tag and ten tags of the wrong name; and git absent, which is
the tool failing and no verdict. The command line's refusals are tests/p5/test_p5_controls.py's and tests/p5kit/test_p5kit_unit.py's.

## Intake, and what a unit states of itself

`python -m qro.p5_unit intake CRATE [--record FILE]` (qro/p5_unit.py; stdlib): (1) the checker's nine steps with every parcel's;
(2) both PUBs of every control job decoded under the Decode's order and judged by `p1_decode.known_answer_verdict` against their
own X sets, a failure refused `known-answer` naming the PUB; (3) `status()`, derived and never stored: `unit-incomplete` (a
planned job of a device's bundle missing, or present with a status file and no result) and `not-interleaved` (the jobs present,
in the order of their execution starts, or of the lines' `submitted_at` for a local unit, do not run the devices K M F three
times). Both are kept, not refused: `KEPT unit-incomplete: fake_fez film-3 missing`, `KEPT not-interleaved: "the difference is the
device's-and-its-time's"`. The first line says the divisor of a scaled unit: `ACCEPTED <digest> unit scaled by shots divisor 128`,
and `ACCEPTED <digest> unit` only for the full recipe, so a unit built with `--shots-divisor` is never taken for a unit by its line
(`Unit.shots_divisor`, `Intake.divisor`; tests/p5/test_p5_unit.py, `test_intake_names_a_scaled_unit_...`). Exit 0 accepted, 1 a refusal, 2 misuse, 3 the tool failing. `--record` writes one canonical JSON line,
`qro-crate/unit-intake/v1`, beside the crate and never in it, and not over a file. Held by tests/p5/test_p5_unit.py, in process and
as a process (exit codes, one line a verdict, a pipe nobody reads and a closed descriptor exit 3 and never 120).

**`qro.p5_unit.read(crate_dir) -> Unit`, the seam P3.2 reads units through** (design item 8), built as the design gives it, from the
crate's metadata alone: `.kind` (from the devices' `qro:deviceKind`), `.recipe_sha256`, `.devices` (in the recipe's order),
`.jobs(device, role, pass_, kind)` (each job with its PUBs, its raw result's path, its line and its time), `.counts(pub_id)`
(through `p1_decode.pub_counts`, so a reader re-derives and never trusts), `.chain(device)`, `.planned(device)`, `.kit_commit`,
`.kit_tag`, `.contributor`, `.clock` and `.status()` (`complete[device]`, `missing`, `interleaved`, `order`; its `lines()` and
`sentences()`). A pair's pool takes only units whose `status().complete[device]` is true for both devices. Held by
tests/p5/test_p5_unit.py (`test_read_*`, `test_a_*_is_kept_as_*`) and, on a real local unit, tests/p5kit/test_p5kit_unit.py
(`test_the_seam_p3_reads_gives_the_real_units_facts`).

## The kit

`python -m qro_kit freeze|drive|crate` (qro_kit/__main__.py): exit 0 done, 1 a refusal as one `REFUSED <name> at <where>: <detail>`
line, 2 misuse, 3 the kit failing (a missing environment is that, and so is git failing in `freeze`'s throwaway repository, `GitFailed`; never a verdict), 4 a drive that stopped after a job was sent.
The kit is a package of its own that `qro` never imports; its module-level imports are the standard library, `qro` and `qro_kit`, and
qiskit and the rest are imported inside functions, after the vendored code is on the path (tests/p5/test_p5_kit_static.py, by
`ast`; and the checker with qiskit, numpy, mpmath and scipy unimportable, as a process).

- `freeze.py`: the bundles (`unit_bundle.py`, adapted from moth-quantum's tools/hw_bundle.py and quantum_film/ibm/bundle.py,
  which are held to v0's plan), the throwaway origin and records tree, `UNIT.json`. A freeze that fails removes what it made
  (qro_kit/crate.py's `Made`, adapted from the adapter's) and nothing else; a directory that holds anything is refused and left.
- `drive.py`: the 18 jobs one at a time through the vendored runner, with the gate (a control job that does not hold on both PUBs
  stops the unit, exit 3, nothing more spent), and the spend and tree rules first. The local job is the dry run's stand-in: an
  id `dryrun-<16 hex>`, a created time, and the raw result as the runtime's own JSON encoding, handed over only once the line is
  anchored. A drive refused before its first job leaves no directory it made.
- `crate.py`: the unit's files, the shipped source circuit and licence, the metadata regenerated by `qro-p5-unit` and written by
  `qro.canonical.write`; then the checker and intake run on what it wrote. A build that fails removes each file and directory it
  made, each with its identity, and nothing else.
- `submit.py`: the spend rules, below, and the one place a sampler is made (`sampler_for`) and the one place it runs (`send`).

### The vendoring

Quantum-Film's runner is carried, not imported: the 22 modules of the closure of `fixer`, `ibm.bundle`, `ibm.runner`,
`ibm.account`, `ibm.keyleak`, `ibm.anchor`, `ibm.decode`, `ibm.raw` and `ibm.isa` (194,424 bytes, pure ASCII, LF only), with its

moth-quantum commit 3a73823. `qro_kit/_qf.py` puts the directory on the path in one place, refuses a different `quantum_film`
already imported, and refuses a changed byte or any file the list does not name (a `.pyd` or a sourceless `.pyc` loads ahead of a source, so not only `.py`). Held by tests/p5/test_p5_vendored.py (a changed byte
fails the stage; the source circuit equals the twin's copy byte for byte), and against a moth-quantum checkout, file by file, by
tests/p5crates/test_p5crates_vendored_bytes.py. NOTICE.md names them. The vendored files pass `lint-kit` as they are. The environment
the kit runs in is qro_kit/environment.lock (the lead's 40 packages, Python 3.12.15); tests/p5kit/test_p5kit_environment.py asserts
its pins first, and that the sandbox's hook is the interpreter's sitecustomize.

### The spend rules, S1 to S7, and the real route

Each is a refusal before a job is sent, shown on a stand-in service, backend and sampler that count every call
(tests/p5/test_p5_spend.py), and at the driver with the vendored runner (tests/p5kit/test_p5kit_drive.py, which asserts that the
count is zero and the unit's directory is byte for byte what `freeze` left).

| rule | refusal |
|---|---|
| S1 one unit, once: a marker or line of this unit, or a commitment of it in a line | `once-only` |
| S2 the instance covers the unit: 216 s and 216 x 0.015434 RU remain | `spend-usage` |
| S3 before a job, charged + the job's estimate <= 270 s; after it, a charge past twice the estimate stops the unit | `spend-unit` |
| S4 a job's cap is a whole number of seconds in 1 to 300, at least its estimate, and the recipe's (15 s, 60 s) | `spend-cap` |
| S5 the instance's plan is `open` | `plan-not-open` |
| S6 the backend object is an IBMBackend from the service, of the manifest's kind and name | `kind-mismatch` |
| S7 the fence: the flag, `--confirm` the unit's first 12 hex digits, a pre-flight record of this unit that holds and is at most now and under 24 hours old (`0 <= now - at < 24 h`; one dated ahead of now is refused and is never "the newest"), a clean tagged tree, S1 to S6 | `route-unsafe` |

`--route real` runs the fence and, in this kit tag, **always ends refused**: with no service the rules cannot be shown, and with a
stand-in service that clears them it stops at "this kit tag builds no connection to IBM". No module of the kit names a service
class, a connection call or a key variable (`test_the_kit_holds_no_way_to_connect_to_ibm`). The acceptor of a pre-flight record is
here (`submit.preflight_ok`); its writer is P5.2's, and **a pre-flight file's name carries no colon** (`<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ>.json`,
under `preflight/` beside the unit directory): the design's `preflight/<UTC>.json` is not a file name on Windows. The runner holds
S1 itself immediately before it writes a job's marker, so `send`, which runs after the marker, does not ask S1 again; the driver
asks it before it makes anything for the job.

## The controls, and the twin

controls/plants_p5.py builds the **unit twin**, a synthetic unit crate (its root says so in its name): P1's twin's kingston
bundle files re-labelled as three devices, the complement's gate list derived from the known answer's by flipping its X set, a
salt and commitment of its own for each of the 54 PUBs, counts drawn by a seeded generator from the law's weights, written in the
crate's own layout and regenerated by `qro-p5-unit`. Variants: a qpu twin (full shots, execution spans), a qpu twin with the owner's
read, blocks of devices, skipped and failed jobs, and a fault in a control job's counts. Section 11's four rows:

| control | planted fault | comes out |
|---|---|---|
| `unit-known-answer` | fez's first control job read its known answer as the complement, every digest consistent | refused `known-answer` by the checker's step 5 (the Decode still claims msb-first) |
| `unit-incomplete` | fez's third film job missing (its bundle still plans it) | accepted; `KEPT unit-incomplete`; `complete = {K: true, M: true, F: false}` |
| `not-interleaved` | the 18 lines in blocks, K x6, M x6, F x6 | accepted; `KEPT not-interleaved` with the study's sentence |
| `kit-dirty` | a throwaway repository with a modified tracked file | `Refused("kit-dirty")` from `tree_state`, the plant's own outcome |

`python -m controls.run` says 22 of 22 built controls come out as section 11 says (tests/p5/test_p5_controls.py,
`test_each_control_comes_out_as_section_11_says`; the twins of the two `states` rows are accepted and say nothing). The same
faults are also made as edits of any built unit crate (`plant_known_answer`, `plant_incomplete`, `plant_interleaved`,
`plant_complement`), which is how tests/p5kit plants them in a real local unit. The complement failing alone is the stated limit
below.

## Measured

- **One film job on FakeKingston** (3 PUBs at 16,384 shots, compiled at level 2 with seed 11, through `SamplerV2` and the local
  runtime service, the kit's interpreter through sbx.sh): **488.9 s**; at 1,024 shots a PUB, 41.9 s. About 9.7 ms a shot and 12 s
  of fixed cost a job. A whole unit at full shots is then about 9 x 489 + 9 x 52 = 4,870 s, BELIEVED from two points.
- **A whole local unit at a shots divisor of 128, through `python -m qro_kit`** (the dev run before the stage): freeze 16 s, drive
  472 s for the 18 jobs (the fixed cost of a job, about 24 s, dominates), crate: 197 files, 1,582,361 bytes, accepted by the checker
  and by intake, with no kept line. tests/p5kit/test_p5kit_unit.py runs this unit on every stage run.
- **The unit at full shots, once, through the CLI** (MEASURED): freeze, drive and crate **7,982.9 s, beside other work** (not an idle
  figure; far above the 4,870 s estimate above), the crate 197 files, 16,201,153 bytes, accepted by the checker and by intake. It
  ran on an earlier tree that lacked the `UserWarning` filter in `qro_kit/__main__.py` and GU9's refusal of a mixed-role read record
  (a local unit carries no read record, so neither changes what the unit does). The lead runs a full-shot unit on the kit as merged.
- **Mutants (round 1):** 132 hand mutants: 114 of the changes, 7 of cleanup (`Made`, `crate`) and 11 of the privacy gate's
  `KNOWN_FILES`, each run against the tests meant to kill it: all killed but one, S4 "at least the
  estimate", which is equivalent (the recipe cap check implies it). Mutants of code that
  writes or deletes ran through sbx.sh alone; the rest through sbx-mid.sh. The drive and freeze cleanup are covered at the `Made`
  level, since a mutant of them needs git's push, which cannot run at low integrity.
- **Mutants (round 2):** 20 hand mutants of round 2's changes, 152 in all with round 1's 132, all 20 killed, run through sbx.sh
  alone. They are 6 of D1 (the pre-flight window), 4 of GU7's status-job check, 2 of the vendoring loader, 2 of `freeze`'s failed
  git, 2 of GU1's cap type and 4 of the scaled unit's line at intake. Three survived the first run (the ahead-skip moved a day,
  GU7's records dropped, the loader's link skip) and each was killed after a test was added for it. One more mutant of D1, which
  drops only the window's lower bound, is equivalent once the ahead-skip is in (no record is then dated ahead of now), so it is
  not among the 20.
- The twin builds in about 2.4 s and the checker takes 2.5 s on it.

## Stated limits, as built

The design's item 14, each with the behaviour it concedes and where it is held:

1. **The crate-only checker accepts a fabricated or resealed unit** (P1.md limit 1): `test_the_real_unit_with_a_resealed_shot_is_accepted_the_crate_only_checkers_stated_limit`.
2. **A tag is local:** a stranger can tag any commit. No test holds it; `test_d6_*` hold the pattern only.
3. **The complement is held only at intake:** a crate whose complement fails and whose known answer holds is accepted by the checker and refused at intake (`test_a_complement_that_fails_alone_is_accepted_by_the_checker_and_refused_at_intake`, and on the real unit).
4. **A local unit tests the pipeline, not the devices:** snapshots dated 2026-04-15 and 2025-02-26, calibration `unexposed`, no execution span, interleaving read from the local `submitted_at` (`test_the_crate_claims_no_anchor_and_no_provider_time`).
5. **A real unit's first charge is unmeasured:** S3's stop after twice the estimate is the response (`test_s3_*`); no real unit ran.
